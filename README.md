_There are many debates over what time actually is._
_Is it a fourth dimension? Is it an illusion? Those are all good guesses, but none of which reflect its true function all that well. A time-stalker's eyes see it differently, you see? The concept of Time begs something far more valuable…_
_To us, Time is an asset._
Your closes friend got you a job at a well-funded (albeit sketchy) institute. Whether you approve of the strange nicety or not, you've got to admit: it's an interesting place. The people are all nice-- far too nice, Flori would say, though you know not to take heed their paranoia nowadays.
